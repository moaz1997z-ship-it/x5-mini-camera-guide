X5 Mini Camera Guide · Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short: this app is an informational guide. It has no sign-in, does not ask for your name, email or phone number, and does not access your camera, microphone, photos or location. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data as described below.

1. About this app

X5 Mini Camera Guide ("the App") is a user guide that explains how to set up and use the X5 Wi-Fi mini camera: setup, Wi-Fi pairing, live view, night vision, motion detection, SD card recording, troubleshooting, and more. The App requires an internet connection to display its content.

2. Information we collect

We do not directly collect, store or sell personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or login is required.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or address.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.
  • We do not receive or store any video or audio from your mini camera.

The only data collected through the App comes from the advertising service described in Section 5.

3. Your camera and its own app

This guide does not control or connect to your camera. Video, recordings and account data handled by the camera's own companion app, cloud service or SD card are governed by the privacy policy of that app or manufacturer, not by this policy.
